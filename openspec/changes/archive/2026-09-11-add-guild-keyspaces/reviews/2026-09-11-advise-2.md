## Independent take (written before design.md)

1. Pin: bc-components derives the X25519 encapsulation half by its
   own `derive_from_key_material`, not by RFC 7748 from an Ed25519
   half. "Wrap-target is the X25519 half on the stored `PublicKeys`"
   means `Wrap.recipient` is a raw X25519 key that is not the §7.2
   conversion of any roster `ed25519` `PublicKey`.
2. Pin: `RosterEntry.puks` admits `alg ∈ {ed25519, p256}` only, at
   least one. A raw X25519 wrap-target has no roster spelling. The
   change must say how a C2 row of this store appears on a roster
   without forking the wire.
3. Pin: `PrivateKeyBase::public_keys()` is `schnorr_public_keys()`.
   `Op.actor` is raw Ed25519. No stored C2 row can be `admin: true`
   or author an op. Say who the day-one house admin is, and that it
   is not our Sign+Auth.
4. Pin: `signup` drops the user `PrivateKeyBase`. A wrap to that
   X25519 half is unwrappable by anyone until Elect export or a
   wallet. The delta needs a scenario, not only prose.
5. Pin: `managed-custody` untouched means join/leave never writes
   the custody document and never calls `authorize_elect`.
6. Pin: §6.1 "applied admin" needs causal replay of the log. No
   SHALL may make the Postgres cache authoritative.
7. Refuse: any new kind, any new `PublicKey.alg` minted here, any
   genesis-id or epoch-merge rule, our Sign+Auth as actor or
   wrap-target, `credentials` rows as PTK targets.
8. Refuse: radio / SSID / Lightning Admin SHALLs in the delta.
9. Tradeoff: wrap-target-only C2 rows keep the wire honest but ship
   an inert keyspace until export. Minting and holding a user
   Ed25519 actor at signup is custody creep against ADR-010. Inert
   is the right side, if the delta says it out loud.
10. Ask: no identikey-log / blake3 / x25519 dep is needed by this
    mapping node; runtime owes the deps.

  an Elect rewrite then `epoch-begin` / `epoch-commit`. Take items

  half", "C1 cannot be a member", "Schnorr cannot author".
- No fork of kinds, genesis id, admin field, or epoch merge.
  `managed-custody` untouched. No `guilds` claim. Radio SHALLs gone.

## Why send-back

**1. The roster entry ADR-012 §4 prescribes cannot be written under
the wire it cites.** §4 says a C2 row of this store "may only be a
wrap-target-only roster entry (`admin: false`, `actor` omitted per
protocol §6)". Protocol §6 `RosterEntry.puks` is `[PublicKey]`,
"at least one", with `alg` `ed25519` or `p256` only. The only C2 PUK
this store produces is a native X25519 key. So the entry has no
lawful `puks`. `Wrap.recipient` (§7) can carry the raw u-coordinate;
`RosterEntry` cannot. The ADR names an errata as "the protocol
repo's box" and no such box exists. An implementer of
`add-keyspace-runtime` reaches this in the first `keyspace.join`
body and must either mint `alg: "x25519"` (fork, take item 7) or
leave `puks` empty (invalid). Both are architecture decisions and
belong here.

**2. The `add-keyspace-runtime` delta still SHALLs the old
curves.** `openspec/changes/add-keyspace-runtime/specs/guild-keyspaces/spec.md:53-54`:
"SHALL treat wrap `recipient` as a member PUK (Ed25519 via X25519,
or P-256)". Under that SHALL the runtime refuses the recipient
ADR-012 §4 mandates. Owed box 3 amended the runtime design.md and
left the delta and proposal ("PUK selector is the user's latest
identity key"). Runtime is ACTIVE BUILD and its tasks say "tests for
the ADDED scenarios"; act would test the wrong curve.

**3. design.md contradicts itself.** Methods table
`design.md:40`: "Wrap-target: user's latest identity key (Ed25519 via
X25519, or P-256)". C2 mapping table, same file: "Not derived via
§7.2". The Methods row is the sentence the prior send-back called
false for every row in this store.

   store waits on it; or amend ADR-012 §4 and the delta to say a C2
   row is a §7 `Wrap.recipient` and not a §6 `RosterEntry` until

   members whose document carries such a key; drop "latest identity
   key" as the selector name.
3. **Fix `design.md:40`** Methods table row to the C2 mapping table's

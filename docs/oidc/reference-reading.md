# OIDC Reference Reading

This repository may be informed by reading established OIDC projects, but it
does not import their source. `ory/fosite` (Apache-2.0),
`panva/node-oidc-provider` (MIT), and `zitadel/oidc` (Apache-2.0) may be read
for reference and must never be pasted into this tree.

Kanidm is MPL-2.0 and remains read-only at a distance. It is not checked out
in this repository, and no source from it is copied here.

Apache-2.0 and MIT works may be read and never pasted. MPL and AGPL works stay
read-only at a distance. This policy does not authorize clones, a vendor
directory, copied files, or source excerpts from any reference project.

## Readings

A later entry may record, in prose only, what was consulted and what was
decided. It must not include a code excerpt.
